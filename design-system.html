Principles

Four rules that explain every decision below.

1 · Editorial, not techy

A light serif carries headlines, and Inter handles everything functional. Headlines use weight 300 and slightly tight tracking.

2 · Flat until it floats

Surfaces use tint, not shadow. Shadows appear only on things that sit above the page: the scrolled header, product mocks and the chat launcher.

3 · Square corners, soft tints

4px is the default radius. The palette is monochromatic: cool neutrals and one blue. Depth comes from tint steps, never from extra hues or rounding.

4 · Motion confirms, never performs

Hovers resolve in 150–200ms. Only reveals and media are slow (700–800ms), and they use expo-out so they settle instead of bouncing.

Color

Monochromatic: cool neutrals plus one blue. Ink is the only text color and blue is the only accent. Green, amber and red appear only as status signals. Tints are backgrounds and never carry text meaning on their own. Click a swatch to copy its token.
